## Historical observation used in the Day 3 drill

| Field | Archived record |
| --- | --- |
| Database | NASA Community Coordinated Modeling Center (CCMC) — DONKI |
| Record | [Auto-generated Space Weather Notification - SEP](https://ccmc.gsfc.nasa.gov/DONKI/view/Alert/47880/1), notification `20260730-AL-005` |
| Event type and ID | Solar Energetic Particle (SEP), `2026-07-30T18:45:00-SEP-001` |
| Threshold crossing | 30 July 2026 at 18:45 UTC |
| Notification issued | 30 July 2026 at 18:53:31 UTC |
| Observation | GOES detected proton flux above 10 particle flux units (pfu) for protons with energies greater than 10 MeV. |

The Day 3 warning and Mission Log use this real observation to explain why a crew would prepare shelter during a solar particle event. The NASA Science / References view gives the observation and its limits. That view and the Habitat science note link to the archived alert. Data credit goes to NASA CCMC DONKI for the notification and to GOES for the observation. [NASA’s explanation of solar particles and shielding](https://science.nasa.gov/science-research/heliophysics/how-nasa-will-protect-astronauts-from-space-radiation-at-the-moon/)

GOES observed particle flux near Earth. A lunar surface radiation dose would require separate measurements or modeling. The game uses this archived alert as historical context and runs its scripted Day 3 storm offline. The in-game day, 65-point storm increase, 20-point shelter effect, and loss threshold are fictional training values independent of the GOES measurement.
